How Seeking Alpha deals are structured

Most confusion comes from treating “a Seeking Alpha deal” as one checkout. It is not. Premium's deal is a first-year research price plus, for eligible new subscribers, a 7-day free trial. Alpha Picks is a different cart item with its own $359 sale price and no trial listed here. The bundle is a combined checkout at $500 for people who want both.
