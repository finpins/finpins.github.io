Shared foundation

Both tiers include unlimited premium articles, earnings call transcripts with audio, Quant Ratings scoring stocks from 1.0 to 5.0 against sector peers on 100+ metrics, factor grades from A+ to F, dividend scores, peer comparisons and ten years of downloadable financials. Our Premium review covers each tool.

PRO's extras

Seeking Alpha's help center lists four: Top Analysts (insights and Top Ideas from its best-performing contributors), Exclusive Coverage (Buy and Strong Buy ideas on stocks with no Wall Street analyst coverage), Upgrades and Downgrades (quant and aggregate analyst rating changes in one place) and Short Ideas with stricter editorial vetting. Coverage of the 2025 holiday sale also mentioned a PRO Quant Portfolio. Features can change, so check the PRO page.

The price gap

$2,400 versus $299 list. Deal sites reported PRO at $1,920 during the 2025 Black Friday sale, with Premium at $239. Even the Premium plus Alpha Picks bundle ($718 first year, $798 separately) sits far below PRO.

A sensible upgrade path

Start on Premium. If you keep noting ideas you wish you had professional-grade sources for, and those ideas could plausibly be worth more than the price difference, the $99 PRO trial month is a reasonable next step. Remember that paid-trial fees are non-refundable.

Feature-by-feature walkthrough

  • Unlimited reading: The one-article monthly cap of the free account disappears; the full contributor library is available.
  • Transcripts with audio: Listen to or read earnings calls in full, useful during reporting season.
  • Sector-relative scoring: The Quant Rating weighs 100+ metrics against same-sector peers and lands on a 1.0–5.0 score.
  • Five grades under the score: Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions each get a letter from A+ to F.
  • Income tools: Dividend safety, growth, yield and consistency scores help screen payers quickly.
  • Data depth: A decade of downloadable financials, peer comparisons, and earnings and dividend forecast visuals.
  • Watchlists that work for you: Portfolio-linked alerts surface rating changes and news on what you own.

Where people go wrong

  • Treating a Strong Buy rating as a guarantee rather than a summary of data.
  • Reading only the articles that agree with you.
  • Overlooking that fees are generally non-refundable after the trial.
  • Choosing a plan for the investor you wish you were, not the one you are.
  • Skipping the checkout check that confirms $269 before paying.

Five questions before you pay

  • Which job am I hiring this for: research, screening, news or picks?
  • Is the free tier of another service or Seeking Alpha already enough?
  • Can I test with a trial or guarantee before committing?
  • What will year two cost?
  • Would I notice if I cancelled?
