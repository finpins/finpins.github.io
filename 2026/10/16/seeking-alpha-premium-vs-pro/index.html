What Premium Includes

Premium unlocks unlimited premium articles from Seeking Alpha's contributor network and earnings call transcripts with audio. Its Quant Rating compares each stock with sector peers on more than 100 metrics and scores it from 1.0 to 5.0, from Strong Sell to Strong Buy, with factor grades from A+ to F for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions. You also get SA stock ratings, author performance, dividend scores, earnings and dividend forecast visuals, peer comparisons and ten years of downloadable financials. For most people managing their own money, that covers the full research workflow. Our Premium review walks through each tool.

What PRO Adds

PRO includes everything in Premium. Seeking Alpha's help center describes four additional areas:

  • Top Analysts: insights and Top Ideas from Seeking Alpha's highest-performing contributors.
  • Exclusive Coverage: Buy and Strong Buy ideas on stocks that have no Wall Street analyst coverage, often smaller or overlooked companies.
  • Upgrades and Downgrades: rating changes from the Quant system and aggregate analyst ratings, gathered in one place.
  • Short Ideas: bearish ideas that go through more rigorous editorial vetting.

Coverage of Seeking Alpha's 2025 holiday sale also described a PRO Quant Portfolio. Details of PRO features can change, so check the current list on Seeking Alpha's PRO page before you buy.

Trials, Renewals and Refunds

Premium gives new subscribers a 7-day free trial. PRO's introductory trial is paid, and Seeking Alpha's policy states that paid-trial fees are non-refundable. More broadly, Seeking Alpha fees are non-refundable unless stated otherwise in writing; subscriptions auto-renew, and you can cancel at any time with cancellation taking effect at the end of the billing period. There is one free trial per service per lifetime. Read how to cancel Seeking Alpha before starting either trial.

Who Actually Needs PRO?

PRO makes sense if your job involves generating investment ideas, or if a single extra idea a year from top analysts, exclusive small-cap coverage or vetted short ideas is worth more than $2,400 to you. That usually means professional money managers, analysts, advisers or very active investors with large portfolios.

For everyone else, the features PRO adds are nice to have rather than essential. Premium's Quant Ratings, factor grades, dividend scores and full analysis library already cover the decisions most individual investors make: what to buy, what to hold and when the story changes.

A Simple Test Before Upgrading

Use Premium for a few months first. Keep a note every time you wish you had something it does not offer: a top analyst's view, coverage of an obscure small cap, a list of rating changes or short ideas. If those notes pile up and the ideas would plausibly be worth more than the $2,100 price difference, PRO's $99 trial month is a reasonable next step. If not, Premium is the right plan.

Seeking Alpha Premium vs Pro: Our Verdict

For self-directed individual investors, Premium is the clear choice in the Seeking Alpha Premium vs Pro decision. It costs $269 in the first year through our link, includes a free trial and covers the research tools that matter most. PRO is a specialist product for professionals, priced at $2,400 a year, and worth it only if its top-analyst ideas, exclusive coverage and short ideas directly feed your work.
