Shared foundation

Both tiers include unlimited premium articles, earnings call transcripts with audio, Quant Ratings scoring stocks from 1.0 to 5.0 against sector peers on 100+ metrics, factor grades from A+ to F, dividend scores, peer comparisons and ten years of downloadable financials. Our Premium review covers each tool.

PRO's extras

Seeking Alpha's help center lists four: Top Analysts (insights and Top Ideas from its best-performing contributors), Exclusive Coverage (Buy and Strong Buy ideas on stocks with no Wall Street analyst coverage), Upgrades and Downgrades (quant and aggregate analyst rating changes in one place) and Short Ideas with stricter editorial vetting. Coverage of the 2025 holiday sale also mentioned a PRO Quant Portfolio. Features can change, so check the PRO page.

The price gap

$2,400 versus $299 list. Deal sites reported PRO at $1,920 during the 2025 Black Friday sale, with Premium at $239. Even the Premium plus Alpha Picks bundle ($718 first year, $798 separately) sits far below PRO.

A sensible upgrade path

Start on Premium. If you keep noting ideas you wish you had professional-grade sources for, and those ideas could plausibly be worth more than the price difference, the $99 PRO trial month is a reasonable next step. Remember that paid-trial fees are non-refundable.

Who should skip it

Skip PRO if you manage a personal portfolio of ordinary size; the extra $2,100 is hard to earn back. Skip Premium too if you only hold index funds.
