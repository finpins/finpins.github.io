Seeking Alpha Pricing (2026 Guide): Premium, Alpha Picks, Bundle & PRO

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Seeking Alpha sells four tiers: a free Basic account, Premium, the Alpha Picks stock-selection service and PRO for professionals, plus a Premium-and-Alpha-Picks bundle. Prices below come from Seeking Alpha's offer pages and help center as of October 2026, with a clear note where a figure comes from third-party deal reports instead.

Quick verdict

  • Premium: $299/yr list, $269 first year via the Premium offer link, 7-day trial.
  • Alpha Picks: $499/yr list, $449 shown via the Alpha Picks offer link (Oct 2026).
  • Bundle: $718 first year ($798 separately).
  • PRO: $2,400/yr; checkout has shown a $99 one-month paid trial.

PRO

PRO is aimed at professional and institutional investors. Checkout has shown an introductory $99 paid trial for one month, after which it renews at $2,400 a year unless cancelled. Compare it in Premium vs PRO.

What past holiday sales looked like

As reported by third-party deal sites: in 2024 Premium was $209, Alpha Picks $359 and the bundle $509; in 2025 Premium was $239, Alpha Picks $399, the bundle $574 and PRO $1,920. Those are history, not a forecast. Black Friday 2026 pricing has not been announced.

Context against rivals

Premium's $299 list price sits near other well-known services: Motley Fool Stock Advisor lists at $199 a year, while Zacks Premium and Morningstar Investor list at $249. See our alternatives guide.

Who should skip it

If you just want to follow a few holdings, the free Basic account (one premium article a month, portfolio tracking, real-time alerts) may be enough, and paying for any tier would be wasted money.
