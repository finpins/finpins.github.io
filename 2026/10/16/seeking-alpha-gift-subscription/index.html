Seeking Alpha Gift Subscription (2026 Guide): How to Gift a Year of Premium

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Looking for a present for someone who invests? Seeking Alpha lets current account holders buy a year of Premium for another person, and the recipient never has to enter a card. Here is how the gift flow works according to Seeking Alpha's support article, what the recipient experiences, and a few details to check before paying.

Quick verdict

  • Gift one year of Premium from Account Settings > Gifts.
  • Recipient needs no credit card.
  • Email can be sent now or scheduled.
  • After twelve months, access ends unless they add payment.

Test Premium for a week

7 days free, then $269 for year one (list $299). No code needed.

Start the free trial →

Step by step

  1. Sign in to your Seeking Alpha account.
  2. Open Account Settings and choose "Gifts".
  3. Press "Gift Now".
  4. Enter the recipient's email and your card, unless one is saved.
  5. Send it straight away or pick a future date.

The recipient gets an email, on the day you choose, inviting them to set up their account.

What lands in their inbox

A year of Premium: unlimited analysis, transcripts with audio, quant ratings and factor grades, dividend grades, screeners, peer comparisons and a decade of financial statements. Full detail in Premium features. No card is required from them, and if they do not add one after the year, Premium simply stops.

What it costs

The support article explains the process but does not quote a separate gift price, so check the figure displayed in the Gifts section. Premium's standard list price is $299. The $269 first-year rate via the Premium offer link is for new subscribers joining through that link; we have not seen it applied to gifts, so do not count on it.

Gift, referral or trial?

  • Gift: the recipient pays nothing for a full year and enters no card.
  • Referral link: free for you; your friend gets 14 days free and you earn $25 credit if they convert. See referral program.
  • Partner offer: they get a free week then $269 for year one via the Premium offer link.

Who it suits

Students on tight budgets (there is no official student rate, see student discount), new graduates opening a first brokerage account, retired relatives managing dividend portfolios, and anyone wary of auto-renewing subscriptions.

Make the gift useful

Include a short note: add your holdings to a portfolio, then check each stock's rating and weakest grade. For an income investor, point to the dividend scorecard; for a student, the call transcripts and financial statements; for a retiree, the alerts on existing holdings.

Practical checks

Confirm the email they actually use. If they already subscribe, ask Seeking Alpha support how a gift would apply before buying. Tell them that access ends after a year unless they add payment, so it does not vanish unexpectedly.

Still deciding?

The trial costs nothing; cancel before day seven if it is not for you.

Alpha Picks and PRO

The gift article covers Premium only. We found no published option to gift Alpha Picks or PRO; ask subscriptions@seekingalpha.com if that is what you want. Holiday shoppers may also want to watch our Black Friday page, though no 2026 offer has been announced.

Premium's features, one by one

  • Contributor analysis: Every premium article in the archive opens up, so you can read the bull case and the bear case on the same ticker instead of one paywalled teaser a month.
  • Earnings call transcripts: Transcripts come with audio, which makes it practical to hear how management answers tough questions rather than relying on a summary.
  • Quant Rating: A single 1.0 to 5.0 score, from Strong Sell to Strong Buy, built by comparing a company with its sector peers on more than 100 metrics.
  • Factor grades: A+ to F grades for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions show what drives the score; weak grades can cap the rating.
  • Dividend scores: Grades for dividend safety, growth, yield and consistency give income investors a fast first check.
  • Financials and comparisons: Ten years of downloadable financials and peer comparison tools let you line companies up side by side.
  • Portfolio alerts: News, rating changes and alerts follow the holdings you add, so changes come to you.

Getting the link price, step by step

  1. Use the Premium offer link to reach checkout.
  2. Create or sign into your account.
  3. Verify the first-year price ($269) and the free week on the summary screen.
  4. Enter a card; nothing is charged during the trial.
  5. Spend the week on stocks you already own.
  6. Keep it, or cancel before day seven to avoid billing.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Treating a Strong Buy rating as a guarantee rather than a summary of data.
  • Reading only the articles that agree with you.
  • Overlooking that fees are generally non-refundable after the trial.
  • Choosing a plan for the investor you wish you were, not the one you are.
  • Skipping the checkout check that confirms $269 before paying.

Your pre-purchase checklist

  • Will I open the tool at least weekly?
  • Have I compared the year-one cost ($269) with the renewal cost ($299)?
  • Do I want research, picks, or both?
  • Have I written down my day-six reminder?
