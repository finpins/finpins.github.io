Four popular research services, four different jobs. Zacks trades on earnings revisions, Morningstar shines for funds and portfolios, the Motley Fool hands you picks, and Seeking Alpha equips you to research individual stocks. There is no universal winner, but there is a best fit for each kind of investor.
