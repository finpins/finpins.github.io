What is inside

Premium supplies the research: contributor analysis, Quant Ratings and factor grades, dividend grades, screeners and portfolio alerts. Alpha Picks supplies the ideas: new picks drawn from stocks rated Strong Buy by the quant system. Seeking Alpha reports Alpha Picks returned +356.93% versus +106.24% for the S&P 500 from July 1, 2022 to October 9, 2026; past performance is no guarantee.

Doing the arithmetic

List prices are $299 for Premium and $499 for Alpha Picks, or $798 together. The bundle is $718 for year one. Bought individually through partner links, Premium is $269 for year one and Alpha Picks is shown at $449 (Oct 2026), so compare the Premium + Alpha Picks bundle link checkout against those two totals before choosing.

After year one

Each service renews at its then-current individual price once the first year ends. Note the renewal date in your account so the second-year bill does not surprise you; our pricing guide lays out every plan.

Holiday timing

Black Friday 2026 pricing has not been announced. If a bundle promotion appears, it will be listed on our Black Friday tracker with a verification date.

Feature-by-feature walkthrough

  • Unlimited reading: The one-article monthly cap of the free account disappears; the full contributor library is available.
  • Transcripts with audio: Listen to or read earnings calls in full, useful during reporting season.
  • Sector-relative scoring: The Quant Rating weighs 100+ metrics against same-sector peers and lands on a 1.0–5.0 score.
  • Five grades under the score: Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions each get a letter from A+ to F.
  • Income tools: Dividend safety, growth, yield and consistency scores help screen payers quickly.
  • Data depth: A decade of downloadable financials, peer comparisons, and earnings and dividend forecast visuals.
  • Watchlists that work for you: Portfolio-linked alerts surface rating changes and news on what you own.

Step by step: starting Premium

  1. Open the Premium offer link in a fresh browser tab.
  2. Sign in, or create a free Seeking Alpha account with your email.
  3. Before typing card details, read the checkout summary: it should show $269 for year one and the 7-day free trial.

  5. Add your holdings to a portfolio straight away so ratings and alerts populate.
  6. Set a phone reminder for day six to keep or cancel.

Where people go wrong

  • Judging by headline percentages instead of the first-year price you will actually pay.
  • Forgetting that Alpha Picks is not part of Premium, then feeling short-changed.
  • Starting the only free trial you will get in a week with no time to use it.
  • Ignoring renewal: the $269 first year renews at $299.
  • Paying for research while holding only index funds.

Five questions before you pay

  • Which job am I hiring this for: research, screening, news or picks?
  • Is the free tier of another service or Seeking Alpha already enough?
  • Can I test with a trial or guarantee before committing?
  • What will year two cost?
  • Would I notice if I cancelled?

Who should skip it

Skip the bundle if you only want one half. Self-directed researchers may be fine with Premium alone via the Premium offer link; hands-off investors who just want picks can take Alpha Picks alone through the Alpha Picks offer link. Small portfolios should also think twice, since the combined fee is a large share of returns.
