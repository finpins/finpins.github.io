TipRanks' Smart Score

A 1–10 score built from eight factors: analysts, insiders, bloggers, investor sentiment, hedge funds, news sentiment, technicals and fundamentals. Most content is aggregated data and expert tracking.

Seeking Alpha's approach

Open a ticker and you see the Quant Rating, five A+ to F factor grades, Wall Street and contributor ratings, bull and bear articles, transcripts with audio, dividend grades and ten years of financials.

Verdict

For forming your own view, Premium. For a quick sense of professional sentiment, TipRanks' expert rankings are genuinely useful.

Feature-by-feature walkthrough

  • Unlimited reading: The one-article monthly cap of the free account disappears; the full contributor library is available.
  • Transcripts with audio: Listen to or read earnings calls in full, useful during reporting season.
  • Sector-relative scoring: The Quant Rating weighs 100+ metrics against same-sector peers and lands on a 1.0–5.0 score.
  • Five grades under the score: Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions each get a letter from A+ to F.
  • Income tools: Dividend safety, growth, yield and consistency scores help screen payers quickly.
  • Data depth: A decade of downloadable financials, peer comparisons, and earnings and dividend forecast visuals.
  • Watchlists that work for you: Portfolio-linked alerts surface rating changes and news on what you own.

Who wins where

Where TipRanks wins: tracking what analysts, insiders, hedge funds and bloggers are doing, and ranking those experts by accuracy, all summarised in a 1–10 Smart Score. Yearly plans carry a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Where Seeking Alpha wins: original analysis, transcripts with audio, five factor grades that explain a rating, dividend scores and ten years of financials — the material you need to form your own view.

Budget-based recommendations

Zero budget: use the free tier of TipRanks where one exists and Seeking Alpha's Basic account, which includes portfolio tracking, real-time alerts and one premium article a month.

One paid tool: pay for the service that matches your main job. If that job is deciding which individual companies to own, Premium at $269 for year one is the natural pick; if it is the specialty TipRanks is known for, choose that.

Two tools: combine a free tier of one with a paid plan of the other, and review after twelve months whether both earned their place. Remember that Premium renews at $299 after year one.
