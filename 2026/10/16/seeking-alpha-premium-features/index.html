The help center adds screeners for stocks and ETFs and AI tools named Virtual Analyst Reports and Earnings Call Insights, plus deep coverage of more than 4,000 stocks and ETFs.

Unlimited reading

Free users get one paywalled article a month. Premium removes that ceiling, so you can line up the optimistic and pessimistic theses on a stock side by side. For most people this is the change they notice first.

Calls, audio and AI summaries

Complete transcripts and recordings let you judge management's answers yourself, and Earnings Call Insights condenses calls with AI. Our transcripts guide shows a quick routine for reporting season.

The quant layer

Each stock is compared with sector peers on more than 100 measures and lands somewhere from Strong Sell to Strong Buy. A+ to F grades for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions explain the verdict. Read how the ratings are built and what the track record shows.

Income tools

Four sector-relative dividend grades cover safety, growth, yield and consistency; Seeking Alpha says the safety grade rests on 13 financial metrics. Details in our dividend grades explainer.

Screeners

Filter by quant, author and Wall Street ratings, factor grades, dividend grades, earnings data and more, then save your screens. Walkthrough: using the stock screener.

Author track records, financials and peers

Author metrics show whose past calls held up. Ten years of statements and peer tables cut spreadsheet work dramatically. The App Store listing also mentions a portfolio health check with warnings, which pairs well with alerts on your holdings.

Virtual Analyst Reports

An AI-written research summary. Useful as a first read on an unfamiliar company, not a substitute for judgement.

What you do not get

Alpha Picks is its own subscription at $499 per year list ($449 via the Alpha Picks offer link as of October 2026). Top Analysts, Exclusive Coverage, daily upgrades and downgrades and vetted Short Ideas belong to PRO at $2,400 per year. Investing Groups are priced individually. If you want research plus picks, Seeking Alpha's help center quotes $718 for year one of the bundle versus $798 bought apart (Premium + Alpha Picks bundle link). See our PRO review.

Matching features to your style

Stock pickers lean on quant grades and analysis; income investors on dividend data; fundamental analysts on transcripts and statements; active traders on rating changes and alerts. If none of these would change a decision, Basic may suffice.

A trial plan

Day one: load your holdings and note each stock's rating and weakest grade. Days two to four: read one bull and one bear piece per holding and check dividend safety where relevant. Day five: decide. Cancel at least a day before the end if it is not for you.

What each Premium tool does

  • The analysis library: Bulls, bears and a comment section on most listed stocks, including smaller companies.
  • Earnings calls: Full transcripts plus audio for each call.
  • The headline score: The Quant Rating, 1.0 to 5.0, compares each stock with its sector across 100+ metrics.
  • What sits underneath: Letter grades for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions, with caps for weak spots.
  • For dividend investors: Scores covering safety, growth, yield and consistency.
  • For number crunchers: Ten years of financials to download and side-by-side peer comparisons.
  • Monitoring: Alerts tied to your portfolio and author ratings to judge contributors.

The earnings reader. Kofi follows thirty companies and listens to calls each quarter; transcripts with audio are his main use.

Step by step: starting Premium

  1. Open the Premium offer link in a fresh browser tab.
  2. Sign in, or create a free Seeking Alpha account with your email.
  3. Before typing card details, read the checkout summary: it should show $269 for year one and the 7-day free trial.
  4. Add a payment method and confirm.
  5. Add your holdings to a portfolio straight away so ratings and alerts populate.
  6. Set a phone reminder for day six to keep or cancel.

Common traps

  • Judging by headline percentages instead of the first-year price you will actually pay.
  • Forgetting that Alpha Picks is not part of Premium, then feeling short-changed.
  • Starting the only free trial you will get in a week with no time to use it.
  • Ignoring renewal: the $269 first year renews at $299.
  • Paying for research while holding only index funds.

Checklist

  • Is my portfolio big enough that the fee is a small percentage?
  • Do I understand what is and is not included?
  • Have I checked the checkout total before entering card details?
  • Do I know how to cancel and when renewal happens?
  • Have I ignored any unverified holiday discount claims?

Who should skip it

Skip Premium if you only hold index funds or check prices occasionally; its tools are built for people choosing individual stocks.
