Seeking Alpha Referral Program (2026 Guide): 14-Day Trial + $25 Credit

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Seeking Alpha runs a refer-a-friend scheme that benefits both sides. Per its help center, a person who signs up through your private link gets fourteen days of Premium free, double the usual trial, and you receive a $25 credit toward your own subscription whenever one of those friends becomes a paying Premium member.

Quick verdict

  • Your friend: 14-day free Premium trial.
  • You: $25 subscription credit per paid conversion.
  • Link location: Account Settings > Referrals.
  • Credits apply to future subscription bills.

7 days free, then $269 for year one (list $299). No code needed.

How it works

  • Share your personal referral link.
  • The friend signs up and gets 14 days of Premium free.
  • If they then buy Premium, you are credited $25.
  • Credits are used against future subscription payments.
  • Seeking Alpha says credits can significantly reduce, or even fully cover, your cost over time.

Finding your link

  1. Sign in.
  2. Click your profile picture, top right.
  3. Choose Account Settings.
  4. Open Referrals.
  5. Copy the link.

Questions go to subscriptions@seekingalpha.com.

How far can credits go?

Four converted friends equal $100 off; twelve equal $300, a little more than Premium's $299 list price. For a member with an active investing club or family, renewal could cost very little.

Fourteen days vs seven

The standard new-member offer is a seven-day trial; a referral link doubles it, enough to cover two weeks of earnings reports. Because Seeking Alpha allows one free trial per service per lifetime, whichever trial someone uses first is the only one they get.

Referral or partner price?

The referral page does not mention the $269 first-year price available through partner links like the Premium offer link. Someone who mostly wants a cheap first year may prefer that route; someone who wants more time to test may prefer the referral. We found nothing saying the two combine.

Comparing the ways in

  • Referral link: 14-day trial; referrer earns $25 if the friend pays.
  • Partner link: seven-day trial and $269 for year one.
  • Gift: a full free year for the recipient, paid by the giver (gift guide).

No codes are needed for partner pricing; see promo code facts.

Good practice

Share with people who will genuinely use it rather than spamming forums. Remind friends to cancel at least a day before their trial ends if they do not want to pay. Keep a note of who you referred and when, and check credits on your invoices or the Referrals page; email support if one is missing.

Still deciding?

The trial costs nothing; cancel before day seven if it is not for you.

Not a subscriber yet?

Premium's features, one by one

  • Contributor analysis: Every premium article in the archive opens up, so you can read the bull case and the bear case on the same ticker instead of one paywalled teaser a month.
  • Earnings call transcripts: Transcripts come with audio, which makes it practical to hear how management answers tough questions rather than relying on a summary.
  • Quant Rating: A single 1.0 to 5.0 score, from Strong Sell to Strong Buy, built by comparing a company with its sector peers on more than 100 metrics.
  • Factor grades: A+ to F grades for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions show what drives the score; weak grades can cap the rating.
  • Dividend scores: Grades for dividend safety, growth, yield and consistency give income investors a fast first check.
  • Financials and comparisons: Ten years of downloadable financials and peer comparison tools let you line companies up side by side.
  • Portfolio alerts: News, rating changes and alerts follow the holdings you add, so changes come to you.

How to sign up correctly

  1. Click through the Premium offer link; typing the address yourself can skip partner pricing.
  2. Log in or register.
  3. Check the total reads $269 for the first year (list $299) and that a 7-day trial is listed.
  4. If it shows $299, reload the link or switch browsers.
  5. Start the trial and build a watchlist on day one.
  6. Decide on day six; cancel from account settings if it is not for you.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Hunting coupon sites for codes the offer does not need.
  • Comparing another service to Seeking Alpha on price alone, without asking which job each tool does.
  • Letting the trial lapse into a paid year by missing day six.
  • Assuming a holiday sale is coming; nothing for 2026 has been announced.
  • Buying the bundle when you will only use one half.

Your pre-purchase checklist

  • Do I buy individual stocks, or plan to within the next few months?
  • Will I open the tool at least weekly?
  • Have I compared the year-one cost ($269) with the renewal cost ($299)?
  • Do I want research, picks, or both?
  • Have I written down my day-six reminder?
