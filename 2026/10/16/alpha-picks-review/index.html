Alpha Picks Review (2026 Guide): +356.93% vs S&P 500, Price & Verdict

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Alpha Picks is Seeking Alpha's curated stock-selection subscription, separate from its Premium research platform. Seeking Alpha reports the portfolio returned +356.93% from July 1, 2022 to October 9, 2026, against +106.24% for the S&P 500 over the same stretch. It lists at $499 a year and is shown at $449 through our offer link (Oct 2026). Below: what you receive, how to read that track record and who should pass.

Quick verdict

  • Reported return: +356.93% vs S&P 500 +106.24% (Jul 2022–Oct 2026, per Seeking Alpha).
  • Price: $449 shown via the Alpha Picks offer link (list $499, Oct 2026).
  • A focused idea list, not a research toolkit.
  • Fee is a high hurdle on small portfolios.

What the service is

Instead of researching hundreds of companies, you get a short list of recommendations drawn from Seeking Alpha's quant rating system, plus updates when positions change. It does not include Premium's research tools; those are a separate subscription.

Fee as a share of your portfolio

At $449 a year, a $10,000 portfolio has to earn roughly 4.5% more than it otherwise would just to cover the cost. On larger portfolios the fee shrinks to a much smaller percentage. Want research as well? Premium alone is $269 for year one through the Premium offer link (list $299, 7-day trial), and the Premium + Alpha Picks bundle link combines both.

Strengths and drawbacks

Good: a strong reported record versus the S&P 500, a short list instead of endless reading, clear updates when picks change.

Less good: $499 list is meaningful money, stock concentration carries more risk than an index fund, and no research tools are included. For a deeper cost-benefit look, see Is Alpha Picks worth it?

What Alpha Picks includes, item by item

  • Twice-monthly picks: New ideas arrive around the 1st and 15th of each month.
  • Quant origin: Picks are drawn from stocks rated Strong Buy by Seeking Alpha's quant system.
  • Full portfolio: Subscribers see current and past picks, not just the latest one.
  • Change alerts: You are notified when positions change.
  • Reported record: Seeking Alpha reports +356.93% vs +106.24% for the S&P 500 from July 1, 2022 to October 9, 2026; past performance does not guarantee future results.
  • What is not included: Premium's research tools; pair them through the bundle if needed.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Treating a Strong Buy rating as a guarantee rather than a summary of data.
  • Reading only the articles that agree with you.
  • Overlooking that fees are generally non-refundable after the trial.
  • Choosing a plan for the investor you wish you were, not the one you are.
  • Skipping the checkout check that confirms $269 before paying.

Your pre-purchase checklist

  • Do I buy individual stocks, or plan to within the next few months?
  • Will I open the tool at least weekly?
  • Have I compared the year-one cost ($269) with the renewal cost ($299)?

  • Have I written down my day-six reminder?

Who should skip it

Passive investors who prefer index funds, and anyone whose portfolio is small enough that $449 would eat a big slice of any gain, should probably pass. So should people who want to research stocks themselves; Premium suits them better.
