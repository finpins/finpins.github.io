What happens when you sign up

The trial sits on the Premium offer. Open the Premium offer link, log in or create an account and add a payment method. You then have seven days of Premium. Do nothing and the plan continues at $269 for the first year; cancel inside the week and you are not billed.

A seven-day test plan

  • Day 1: load your holdings into a portfolio so ratings and alerts appear.
  • Days 2–3: read the analysis and Quant Ratings on what you own.
  • Day 4: run a screen on an idea you have been sitting on.
  • Day 5: compare two stocks side by side.
  • Day 6: keep or cancel.

If you only logged in once, that is your answer.

Stopping before the charge

Cancel from account settings, ideally on day six rather than the last day so time zones cannot trip you up. Our cancellation guide covers the full rules, including what happens after a paid period starts.

Trials on other products

The week-long trial belongs to Premium. Alpha Picks is a separate service at $499/yr list, shown at $449 via the Alpha Picks offer link as of Oct 2026; the Premium + Alpha Picks bundle link combines both. If you are unsure which you need, read Premium vs Alpha Picks first.

Premium's features, one by one

  • Contributor analysis: Every premium article in the archive opens up, so you can read the bull case and the bear case on the same ticker instead of one paywalled teaser a month.
  • Earnings call transcripts: Transcripts come with audio, which makes it practical to hear how management answers tough questions rather than relying on a summary.
  • Quant Rating: A single 1.0 to 5.0 score, from Strong Sell to Strong Buy, built by comparing a company with its sector peers on more than 100 metrics.
  • Factor grades: A+ to F grades for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions show what drives the score; weak grades can cap the rating.
  • Dividend scores: Grades for dividend safety, growth, yield and consistency give income investors a fast first check.
  • Financials and comparisons: Ten years of downloadable financials and peer comparison tools let you line companies up side by side.
  • Portfolio alerts: News, rating changes and alerts follow the holdings you add, so changes come to you.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Signing up by typing the URL, which can bypass link pricing.
  • Expecting PRO-level features from Premium, or paying $2,400 for PRO before testing Premium.
  • Measuring a picks fee in dollars rather than as a share of your portfolio.
  • Not adding holdings on day one, which makes the trial feel empty.
  • Subscribing to two overlapping tools when one free tool would do.

Your pre-purchase checklist

  • Do I buy individual stocks, or plan to within the next few months?
  • Will I open the tool at least weekly?
  • Have I compared the year-one cost ($269) with the renewal cost ($299)?
  • Do I want research, picks, or both?
  • Have I written down my day-six reminder?
