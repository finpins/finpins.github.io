What the Premium notice actually says

Seeking Alpha's 'Premium Subscription Price Update' page tells subscribers the increase starts with their next renewal, when the plan renews automatically at $299 a year plus sales tax or VAT where applicable. It reminds them they can cancel in Subscription Settings beforehand and gives a customer service number, 1-347-509-6837.

Can they change your price?

Yes. Article 20 of the Terms of Use reserves Seeking Alpha's right to revise fees on reasonable notice. In the notices we found, new prices apply at the next renewal, leaving time to decide.

Ways to pay less now

  • New to Premium: $269 for the first year through a partner link, after a free week.
  • New to Alpha Picks: $449 via our offer link instead of $499.
  • Want both: the bundle is $718 in year one per Seeking Alpha's help center, versus $798 separately (Premium + Alpha Picks bundle link).
  • Existing members: each friend who converts after a 14-day referral trial earns you a $25 credit (referral program).
  • Seasonal sales: Seeking Alpha has run temporary promotions before, but nothing for Black Friday 2026 had been announced by October 16 (Black Friday page).

Renew or walk away?

Look at how often you used the service. Weekly use on a meaningful portfolio probably still justifies $299. Rare use suggests cancelling before renewal; you keep access to the end of the term. Remember that once a renewal is charged it is generally non-refundable. Steps: cancel Seeking Alpha.

Plan for renewal on day one

When you join at $269, diarise the renewal date with the $299 list price and set a reminder two weeks earlier. That turns a surprise into a decision.

Future increases

We have no information on further changes and will not speculate. So far the pattern has been advance notice followed by the new price at renewal. Current list prices are summarised in Seeking Alpha pricing.

How to sign up correctly

  1. Click through the Premium offer link; typing the address yourself can skip partner pricing.
  2. Log in or register.
  3. Check the total reads $269 for the first year (list $299) and that a 7-day trial is listed.
  4. If it shows $299, reload the link or switch browsers.
  5. Start the trial and build a watchlist on day one.
  6. Decide on day six; cancel from account settings if it is not for you.

Common traps
