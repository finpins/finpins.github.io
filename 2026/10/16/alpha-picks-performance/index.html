Who reports it

Seeking Alpha publishes the figure for the whole portfolio since inception, closed positions included. We know of no third-party audit, so read it as a credible company claim. Past results never guarantee future ones.

What drives the returns

Eligibility, per the help center: Strong Buy for at least 75 straight days, US common stock only (no ADRs), no REITs, three-month average market value above $500 million, price above $10, and not chosen in the previous twelve months. One stock is added on the first trading day of each month and one on or after the 15th, and members get Hold and Sell alerts. Mechanics: how Alpha Picks works.

Why your result will differ

A subscriber who joins in late 2026 does not inherit 2022's entry prices. Skipping picks, buying days later or sizing positions differently all change the outcome. Holding picks in a taxable account adds capital-gains drag the reported figure ignores.

Concentration cuts both ways

A list of a few dozen stocks can be carried by a handful of big winners. That helps in good periods and can hurt in bad ones; expect bigger swings than an index fund.

The period matters

The record starts near the 2022 bear-market lows and includes a strong recovery. Other market environments may favour different factors.

Reading member updates critically

When performance updates arrive, check how many picks are beating the index since their own entry date, how dependent the total is on the top two or three names, and how closed positions fared. Members can see the whole portfolio, so this is easy to do.
