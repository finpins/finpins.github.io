How to Cancel Alpha Picks (2026 Guide): Steps, Refunds and What You Keep

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Stopping Alpha Picks takes about two minutes in Account Settings. The part people get wrong is timing and expectations: cancellation switches off renewal at the end of the current term, and Seeking Alpha's policy treats fees already paid as non-refundable. Here is the process and the fine print, taken from Seeking Alpha's support center and terms.

Quick verdict

  • Cancel under Account Settings or by emailing subscriptions@seekingalpha.com.
  • Access continues until the paid term ends.
  • No refund for unused time under the standard policy.
  • Keeping Premium? That is allowed.

The steps

  1. Sign in on seekingalpha.com.
  2. Click your avatar, then Account Settings.
  3. Open your subscription list (press "Show Details" if collapsed).
  4. Select Alpha Picks and cancel.
  5. Save the confirmation message.

The same page shows trial end, next charge date and renewal amount, so confirm them afterwards. If you bought Alpha Picks inside the iPhone app, where it appears as a $499.00 purchase, Apple bills you and you cancel through your Apple ID subscriptions.

What happens next

You can cancel whenever you like. It takes effect when the current billing period finishes, you keep full access until then, and nothing renews. Every Seeking Alpha subscription auto-renews unless you do this. Fees are non-refundable unless stated otherwise in writing; the only written 30-day guarantee covers first-time annual Investing Group members. See the refund policy.

Keeping Premium

Seeking Alpha confirms that Premium and Alpha Picks stand alone, so dropping one does not break the other. The cancellation applies at renewal. Bundle buyers should note that renewals are charged at each product's then-current individual price, so check Premium's renewal amount ($299 list today) after you change anything.

Before you click cancel

First, decide how you will manage picks you still hold, because Hold and Sell alerts stop when access ends; Premium's quant ratings are one way to keep watching them. Second, compare the fee with your allocation: $449 to $499 is trivial on a large portfolio and heavy on a small one (worked example). Third, ask whether Premium alone at $269 for year one via the Premium offer link covers what you actually use; see Premium vs Alpha Picks.

Coming back later

You can resubscribe whenever you like at the price then in force: $499 list now, $449 through the Alpha Picks offer link as of October 2026. Free trials are limited to one per service per lifetime.

Unexpected charge?

Write to Seeking Alpha support at subscriptions@seekingalpha.com with the invoice, or call the number on Seeking Alpha's price notices, 1-347-509-6837. Anything beyond the written policy is discretionary.

Cancel smart

The cheapest moment to cancel is shortly before renewal, after you have had full use of the term you paid for. Put the renewal date in your calendar with a two-week reminder.

The trial costs nothing; cancel before day seven if it is not for you.

How to sign up correctly

  1. Click through the Premium offer link; typing the address yourself can skip partner pricing.
  2. Log in or register.
  3. Check the total reads $269 for the first year (list $299) and that a 7-day trial is listed.
  4. If it shows $299, reload the link or switch browsers.
  5. Start the trial and build a watchlist on day one.
  6. Decide on day six; cancel from account settings if it is not for you.

Where people go wrong

  • Hunting coupon sites for codes the offer does not need.
  • Comparing another service to Seeking Alpha on price alone, without asking which job each tool does.
  • Letting the trial lapse into a paid year by missing day six.
  • Assuming a holiday sale is coming; nothing for 2026 has been announced.
  • Buying the bundle when you will only use one half.

Five questions before you pay

  • Which job am I hiring this for: research, screening, news or picks?
  • Is the free tier of another service or Seeking Alpha already enough?
  • Can I test with a trial or guarantee before committing?
  • What will year two cost?
  • Would I notice if I cancelled?
