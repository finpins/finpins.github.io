Is It Worth $449?

Think of the fee as a percentage of your portfolio. On a $10,000 portfolio, $449 is about 4.5% per year, a high hurdle. On $100,000, it is about 0.45%. Alpha Picks makes more sense the larger the share of your portfolio you put into individual stocks, and if you are comfortable with the extra risk of concentrated positions.

How to Use Alpha Picks After You Subscribe

Getting the price right is only the first step. To get value from the service:
