Seeking Alpha Annual vs Monthly (2026 Guide): $269/$299 a Year vs $49 a Month

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Billing frequency changes the real cost of Seeking Alpha Premium more than most people realise. The website sells Premium as a yearly plan at $299, cut to $269 for year one through the Premium offer link, with a free week up front. Apple's App Store lists a Premium Monthly in-app purchase at $49.00. Here is what that means for your wallet.

Quick verdict

  • 12 months at $49 = $588.
  • Annual list = $299; year one via our link = $269.
  • Monthly passes the $269 offer during month six.
  • Neither option is refundable once billed.

Side-by-side cost

  • Annual, partner first year: $269 (about $22.42 a month).
  • Annual, list: $299 (about $24.92 a month).
  • Monthly via iOS in-app purchase: $49.00 a month, $588 over a year.

Seeking Alpha's help center describes Premium as costing $299 a year. Its cancellation policy mentions monthly and annual plans, so monthly billing exists in some channels, but availability can vary by platform and offer; check the price at checkout.

The break-even point

Six months at $49 is $294, already more than the $269 first-year deal; seven months ($343) passes $299 list. So if you expect to keep Premium for half a year or longer, annual wins. Monthly only saves money for short stints of one to five months, such as one earnings season or a coursework project.

Cancelling and refunds

Seeking Alpha treats both the same: cancel any time, effective at the end of the current period, with fees non-refundable unless agreed in writing. Monthly billing simply means the current period is shorter, giving more flexibility. Details: refund policy.

Apple vs website

Buy through the iPhone app and Apple handles billing and cancellation via your Apple ID. Buy on the web and you manage it in Seeking Alpha's Account Settings. Either way, your login unlocks Premium on web and app. See our app review.

Extra costs to factor in

Seeking Alpha's notices mention sales tax or VAT where applicable. Non-US cards may add currency conversion fees. On monthly billing those extras recur twelve times.

Which suits you

  • Annual: you research individual stocks year-round and want the lowest effective monthly cost.
  • Monthly: you need Premium for a defined short project and monthly billing is offered to you; set a reminder to stop.
  • Either: start with the free trial, remembering it is one per service per lifetime.

Alpha Picks and PRO billing

Alpha Picks is an annual product at $499 list ($449 via the Alpha Picks offer link in October 2026), also listed at $499.00 in the App Store. PRO is $2,400 a year. We found no monthly Alpha Picks plan. For the full menu, see Seeking Alpha pricing and price increase history.

Where people go wrong

  • Signing up by typing the URL, which can bypass link pricing.
  • Expecting PRO-level features from Premium, or paying $2,400 for PRO before testing Premium.
  • Measuring a picks fee in dollars rather than as a share of your portfolio.
  • Not adding holdings on day one, which makes the trial feel empty.
  • Subscribing to two overlapping tools when one free tool would do.

Five questions before you pay

  • Which job am I hiring this for: research, screening, news or picks?
  • Is the free tier of another service or Seeking Alpha already enough?
  • Can I test with a trial or guarantee before committing?
  • What will year two cost?
  • Would I notice if I cancelled?
