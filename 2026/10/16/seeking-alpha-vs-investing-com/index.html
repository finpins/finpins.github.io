InvestingPro

Fair value estimates from several models, financial health scores, historical financials, advanced screeners and AI-selected strategies. Third-party reviews report about $17.95/mo or $114/yr for Pro and $44.95/mo or $293.40/yr for Pro+ in the US.

Seeking Alpha Premium

Unlimited analysis and transcripts with audio, Quant Ratings 1.0–5.0 with five factor grades, dividend scores, peer comparisons and ten years of financials. After the 7-day trial, fees are generally non-refundable, but you can cancel any time and keep access to period end.

Verdict

Keep Investing.com's free site for global quotes and the calendar; pay for Seeking Alpha for US stock research.

What each Premium tool does

  • The analysis library: Bulls, bears and a comment section on most listed stocks, including smaller companies.
  • Earnings calls: Full transcripts plus audio for each call.
  • The headline score: The Quant Rating, 1.0 to 5.0, compares each stock with its sector across 100+ metrics.
  • What sits underneath: Letter grades for Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum and EPS Revisions, with caps for weak spots.
  • For dividend investors: Scores covering safety, growth, yield and consistency.
  • For number crunchers: Ten years of financials to download and side-by-side peer comparisons.
  • Monitoring: Alerts tied to your portfolio and author ratings to judge contributors.

If money is tight

Zero budget: use the free tier of InvestingPro where one exists and Seeking Alpha's Basic account, which includes portfolio tracking, real-time alerts and one premium article a month.

One paid tool: pay for the service that matches your main job. If that job is deciding which individual companies to own, Premium at $269 for year one is the natural pick; if it is the specialty InvestingPro is known for, choose that.

Two tools: combine a free tier of one with a paid plan of the other, and review after twelve months whether both earned their place. Remember that Premium renews at $299 after year one.

Common traps

  • Judging by headline percentages instead of the first-year price you will actually pay.
  • Forgetting that Alpha Picks is not part of Premium, then feeling short-changed.
  • Starting the only free trial you will get in a week with no time to use it.
  • Ignoring renewal: the $269 first year renews at $299.
  • Paying for research while holding only index funds.

Checklist

  • Is my portfolio big enough that the fee is a small percentage?
  • Do I understand what is and is not included?
  • Have I checked the checkout total before entering card details?
  • Do I know how to cancel and when renewal happens?
  • Have I ignored any unverified holiday discount claims?
