How to Cancel Seeking Alpha (2026 Guide): Subscription, Trial & Refund Rules

By FinPins · October 16, 2026

Cancelling Seeking Alpha is easy; getting money back usually is not. Subscriptions renew automatically and fees are non-refundable unless Seeking Alpha says otherwise in writing, but you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Here is the process for Premium, Alpha Picks and trials, based on Seeking Alpha's help center.

Quick verdict

  • Cancel anytime from account settings; access lasts to period end.
  • Fees are generally non-refundable; paid-trial fees too.
  • One free trial per service per lifetime.
  • App-store purchases are cancelled through the store.

Steps

  1. Sign in on the web.
  2. Open account settings, then subscriptions.
  3. Pick the service (Premium, Alpha Picks or another) and cancel or switch off auto-renewal.
  4. Keep the confirmation email.

Menu labels change, so follow the current help center wording if something looks different. Bought through an app store? Cancel there.

During the free week

Cancel before day seven and you are not charged. Since you only get one trial per service, start it in a week you will actually use it; our free trial guide has a plan.

Alpha Picks and the bundle

Same rules: cancelling stops renewal and access continues to the end of the period. With the bundle, check how each service appears in your account; after year one each renews at its then-current individual price.

Before you hit cancel

Note the renewal date, as cancelling early brings no benefit. Check you are on the right plan: some people drop Premium when they really wanted picks, or vice versa. If cost is the issue, remember the $269 first year renews at $299. Returning later? Premium via the Premium offer link is $269 for a first year.

Who should skip it

This section is for readers deciding not to cancel: if you open Seeking Alpha several times a week, cancelling to save $299 may cost you more in missed research than it saves.

Questions readers ask

Can I cancel any time?

Yes; access continues until the period you paid for ends.

Will I get a refund?

Generally no; fees are non-refundable unless stated otherwise in writing.

How do I avoid being charged after the trial?

Cancel before the seven days run out.

Can I take another free trial later?

No, one free trial per service per lifetime.

I subscribed via an app store. What now?

Cancel through that store's subscription settings.
