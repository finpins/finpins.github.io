How to Cancel Seeking Alpha Step by Step

Cancellation is handled from your Seeking Alpha account. In general terms:

  • Sign in to Seeking Alpha on the web.
  • Open your account settings and go to the subscriptions section.
  • Select the subscription you want to stop (Premium, Alpha Picks or another service) and choose the option to cancel or turn off auto-renewal.
  • Confirm, and keep the confirmation email for your records.

If you subscribed through an app store, you may need to cancel through that store's subscription settings instead. If you cannot find the option, Seeking Alpha's cancellation and refund policy page and support team are the official sources. Menu labels change over time, so follow the current help center instructions.

Cancelling During the Free Trial

New Premium subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you decide Premium is not for you, cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Because Seeking Alpha allows only one free trial per service per lifetime, it pays to plan your trial for a week when you will actually use it. Our free trial guide has a day-by-day plan.

Cancelling After You Have Paid

Once a paid period starts, cancelling stops the next renewal, but you keep access until the end of the current period. Seeking Alpha's policy is that fees are non-refundable unless otherwise stated in writing, so cancelling mid-year generally does not produce a partial refund. If you believe you have a special case, contact support, but do not count on a refund.

Cancelling Alpha Picks or the Bundle

Alpha Picks follows the same rules: cancel to stop renewal and keep access until the end of the period. If you bought the Premium plus Alpha Picks bundle, check how each service is listed in your account; after the first year each renews at its then-current individual price.

Before You Cancel: Three Things to Check

1. Your renewal date. Since access continues until the end of the period, there is no benefit to cancelling early beyond making sure you do not forget. Note the date and cancel any time before it.

2. Whether you are using the right plan. Some people cancel Premium when what they really wanted was curated ideas (Alpha Picks), or cancel Alpha Picks when they mainly use research tools (Premium). Compare in Seeking Alpha Premium vs Alpha Picks.

3. Whether the free account is enough. After cancelling, your account reverts to Basic, which still includes portfolio tracking, real-time alerts and one premium article a month. See Seeking Alpha free vs Premium.

Getting More Value Instead of Cancelling

If cost is the issue, note that the $269 first-year price renews at $299. Some investors find that once they build a routine, checking quant ratings on holdings weekly, reading earnings transcripts after results and using dividend scores to review income stocks, the subscription pays for itself. If you only log in occasionally, cancelling and returning later may be smarter.

Will Seeking Alpha Offer a Discount to Stay?

We have not verified any formal retention offer, so we do not promise one. Seasonal sales such as Black Friday have historically offered lower prices to new subscribers; see Seeking Alpha Black Friday deal.

Keep a Record

Whenever you change a subscription, save the confirmation email and note the date. If a renewal charge appears unexpectedly, that record makes any conversation with support much quicker.

How to Cancel Seeking Alpha: Summary

To cancel Seeking Alpha, turn off auto-renewal for the relevant subscription in your account settings (or through the app store if you subscribed there) before your renewal date. You keep access until the period ends, but fees are generally non-refundable. During the free trial, cancel before day seven to avoid a charge.

How to Cancel Seeking Alpha FAQ

Can I cancel Seeking Alpha any time?

Yes. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access until then.

Does Seeking Alpha give refunds?

Its policy is that fees are non-refundable unless stated otherwise in writing. A 30-day limited money-back guarantee applies only to first-time annual Investing Group subscriptions.

How do I avoid being charged after the free trial?

Cancel before the 7-day Premium trial ends.

What happens to my account after cancelling?

It reverts to the free Basic account, which keeps portfolio tracking, real-time alerts and one premium article per month.

Can I get another free trial later?

No. Seeking Alpha allows one free trial per service per lifetime.
