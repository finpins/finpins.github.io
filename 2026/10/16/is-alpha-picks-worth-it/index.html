Strengths on each side

When the answer is yes: you hold a meaningful sum in individual stocks, you can tolerate a concentrated list falling behind the index for a year or more, and you will actually act on two picks a month.

When the answer is no: your stock allocation is small, you would rather own index funds, or you prefer forming your own views, in which case Premium's $269 first year is the better tool. Revisit the question each renewal by comparing the fee with your portfolio's size at that time.
